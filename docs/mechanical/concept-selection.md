# R02 industrial-design concept selection

| Concept | Structural / manufacturing | visibility / access / guarding | service / footprint / visual coherence | score / decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Compact arch | Strong bridge but requires deep formed structure and obscures rear service | good chamber, constrained upper access | larger shell / generic appliance risk | 23/35 - not selected |
| Swept side supports | Folded planar supports plus rear spine provide clear chamber and rigid load paths | excellent front deck access; upward guard has clear hinge line | panels/trim replaceable; compact 560 x 430 footprint | 30/35 - **selected** |
| Cantilevered upper structure | clean front access but high spine moment and vibration uncertainty | clear front, but harder cable/guard integration | higher precision/stiffness burden | 24/35 - not selected |

R02 uses warm light-gray folded external panels, graphite spine/plinth, satin precision head components, and restrained cyan fixture/status accents. The shape derives from front load clearance, guard sweep, rear cable routing, and side load paths rather than decorative sculpting.
