## Why acquisition beats greenfield

Reproducing `loomforge` means rediscovering domain constraints, re-implementing architecture and edge cases, rebuilding tests/CI/packaging, and rewriting diligence materials already present in this repository. Buying the transferable IP is the faster path to a production-ready starting point.
