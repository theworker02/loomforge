# Existing systems and proposed position

Komax markets Omega 740/750/840/850 platforms for automated harness assembly; its Omega 850 states insertion of individual wires and twisted wires. Schleuniger and TE/Molex application tooling address production-scale wire processing and termination. These systems establish that automated insertion and traceability are existing capabilities; LoomForge makes no novelty claim.

LoomForge's proposed differentiation is a compact, supervised, one-housing bench cell using keyed, front-access fixture cartridges and declarative recipes to reduce setup burden for prototypes and small batches. It deliberately excludes wire cutting, stripping, crimping, loose-wire picking, and unattended operation.

The initial design is not performance-comparable with industrial production equipment. Any claim about changeover time or yield awaits physical trials.
