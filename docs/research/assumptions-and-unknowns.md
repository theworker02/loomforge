# Assumptions and unknowns

| Item | Value / status | Classification | Next action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Working envelope | 260 x 220 x 300 mm | Calculated from conceptual travel/service clearance | Layout review with purchased rails and fixture sample |
| X/Y/Z travel | 180 / 100 / 80 mm | Proposed | Prove reach and collision clearance in CAD with controlled STEP data |
| force sensor | 0–50 N, overload stop at 15 N process limit | Proposed | Select calibrated sensor, characterize noise and overload protection |
| guide-to-cavity unsupported length | <=12 mm | Assumed | Bench buckle study with worst-case 24 AWG wire |
| position repeatability | <=0.10 mm target | Proposed | Laser/dial-indicator repeatability study |
| insertion speed | 10 mm/s target | Proposed | Establish with terminal/housing trials |
| mechanical seating | force/travel + fixture datum | Proposed | Compare against supplier-approved inspection and pull test |
| electrical test | 5 V maximum, 2 mA maximum | Proposed | Verify leakage, switching resistance, fault protection |

Unknown manufacturer geometry, terminal orientation keying, insulation envelope, required terminal seating depth, and secondary-lock applicability shall block fixture release. The repository does not substitute web excerpts for controlled drawings.
