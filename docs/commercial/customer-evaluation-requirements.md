# Customer evaluation requirements

A prospective receiving manufacturer should evaluate LoomForge only against a defined manual or existing-equipment baseline for a selected connector/recipe. Required evidence: controlled component documentation; fixture/guide drawings; parts substitution review; force/displacement calibration; repeatability data; damage and seating inspection; electrical-test uncertainty and fault coverage; safety assessment; recovery behavior; record/export review; and documented operator/maintenance training.

Proposed metrics: fixture changeover time, setup time per supported recipe, cycle time, first-pass assembly success, terminal/housing damage rate, fault detection and false-rejection rates, interventions per assembly, and cost per completed assembly. Savings, ROI, demand, and valuation are intentionally not estimated.
