# Bottleneck evidence and evaluation framing

LoomForge addresses proposed workflow problems: repetitive manual insertion, wrong cavities/incomplete seating, fixture changeover, small-run setup cost, disconnects between designs/instructions/records, late error discovery, and revision traceability. It does not claim measured improvement.

| Workflow problem | Organization types with public relevance | Evidence | Proposed LoomForge response | Measurement needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Manual work and preventable wiring errors | control-panel builders, electronics manufacturers | Siemens says its panel engineering content targets reducing manual work/preventing errors; Jabil describes automated wiring and traceability. Links in target matrix. | declarative recipes, keyed fixture, bounded insertion, test record | manual baseline, first-pass success, damage/fault detection and false reject rates |
| Variant/changeover work | prototype/equipment teams, CM/NPI teams | Tesla/Rivian public material establishes prototype/manufacturing equipment activity, not a bottleneck. | keyed cartridge and recipe/fixture identity | timed changeover and recipe setup vs defined baseline |
| Traceability/test correlation | EMS and regulated engineering organizations | Jabil and Flex materials state traceability requirements/capabilities. | persisted job, calibration, force trace and test result | audit review, record completeness and mapping accuracy |

Existing solutions include vendor insertion tooling, dedicated harness-production platforms, design-to-manufacturing suites, interactive guidance, and internal manufacturing equipment. Integration barriers include exact connector qualification, safety, plant data interfaces, cybersecurity, existing capital equipment, calibration, and factory acceptance procedures.
