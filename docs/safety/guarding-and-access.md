# Guarding and operator access

R02 selects an upward-hinged front polycarbonate guard (proposed 6 mm) over a vertically lifting door: it preserves bench-front clearance, opens the front deck without a lower sill, and can share a clear upper hinge line with the side supports. It introduces overhead sweep, hinge/strut pinch hazards, and retention requirements. Final material, thickness, attachment spacing, impact resistance, hinges, stays, and interlock category require qualified safety review.

The guard has a full-width handle outside the chamber, mechanical opening stop at 105 degrees, retained fasteners, and dual-channel interlock mounted at the right support/spine. Opening requests controller stop and safety-chain response; closing does not restart. The E-stop is on the right edge of the angled console and remains reachable with the door open. Fixture/tray remove forward after guard opening; electronics service is rear-panel-only with power isolation.
