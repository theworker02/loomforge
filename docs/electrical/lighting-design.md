# Lighting design (R02)

| Zone | Purpose / mounting | proposed electrical / control | isolation and service |
|---|---|---|---|
| Work illumination | two diffuse neutral-white bars below upper spine, aimed at deck | 24 V, 12 W total, dimmable constant-current modules | separate baffle from camera; replace from rear service panel |
| Vision illumination | short directional/diffuse-selectable bar adjacent to camera with shroud | 24 V, 6 W, exposure-synchronized controller output | status light disabled/held during capture; characterize glare/exposure |
| Status guide | segmented cyan/amber/red light guide in spine, paired with console text/status tower | 24 V, 3 W maximum | not a safety signal; replace from rear panel |

Proposed meanings: ready teal, running teal steady, waiting white/console text, completed green plus text, fault/protective stop red plus audible/text indicator, and simulation mode cyan UI banner. Final colours must be reviewed against site conventions and applicable guidance; color alone is never sufficient. Drivers are fused branches, wiring follows rear spine, and thermal rise/brightness/camera interaction require test.
