# Build-readiness checklist

## SC-A0 / proposed M1 pilot supplement

For the proposed 12-unit manufacturer pilot, use the [SC-A0 manufacturing plan](sc-a0-manufacturing-plan.md), [M1 pilot traveler](m1-pilot-traveler.md), [DFM risk register](dfm-risk-register.md), [supplier RFQ package](supplier-rfq-package.md), and [design verification plan](design-verification-plan.md) alongside this checklist. These documents organize supplier review and controlled prototype evidence; they do not change the package to production-ready or authorize procurement.

Do not label the package ready-to-manufacture. Before a prototype build, the receiving team must close each applicable item:

- [ ] Controlled drawings/revisions for housing `39-01-2040`, terminal `39-00-0038`, and mating test interface reviewed.
- [ ] Guide, fixture and rail critical dimensions/tolerance stack measured and released by responsible engineer.
- [ ] Purchased motion, force sensor, safety relay, PSU, guarding and connectors selected with datasheets.
- [ ] Electrical schematic, wire gauge, branch protection, grounding, EMC and enclosure thermal design reviewed.
- [ ] Risk assessment/safeguarding architecture reviewed by qualified machinery-safety authority.
- [ ] CAD exports/drawings regenerated and inspected on an available CAD workstation.
- [ ] Prototype insertion/retention and test-fixture studies completed with raw evidence.
- [ ] Calibration, FAT/SAT, maintenance and training plans approved for intended site.

Current maturity: simulation software—prototype demonstrator; recipe/state logic—tested simulation; conceptual CAD—development source only; mechanical guide/fixture—experimental; electrical architecture—preliminary; safety—concept only; production readiness—not established.
