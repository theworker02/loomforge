# DFM risk register — SC-A0 / proposed M1 pilot

**Document:** LF-MFG-RSK-001
**Revision:** A0 (proposed)
**Status:** active engineering-development register; not a production-process approval

The risks below are deliberately framed as fabrication and assembly concerns. Ratings are qualitative until the receiving manufacturer selects processes, suppliers, and inspection capability. “Closure evidence” means evidence needed to reduce the risk; it is not claimed to exist in this package.

## Risk handling rules

- A risk that can affect safeguarding, unexpected motion, terminal/housing damage, or test disposition is a **build hold** until a manufacturer-authorized temporary control exists.
- A pilot observation must be added to this register or a linked nonconformance record; do not normalize recurring rework as an operator technique.
- Closure of a DFM item requires a configuration-controlled drawing/BOM update where the evidence changes the design.
- This register does not substitute for a manufacturer’s process FMEA, machinery safety assessment, supplier qualification, or application-specific compliance review.
