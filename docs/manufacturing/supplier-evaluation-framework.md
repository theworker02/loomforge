# Supplier evaluation framework — proposed M1 pilot

Supplier names, ratings, quotations, and approval status are intentionally absent. The receiving manufacturer owns qualification.

| Criterion | Evidence sought | Weight (proposed) | Disqualifying condition |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Technical capability | comparable materials/processes, tolerance evidence, inspection plan | 25% | cannot inspect critical features |
| Quality and traceability | lot control, NCR response, revision control | 20% | refuses revision/lot traceability for critical parts |
| Schedule realism | lead-time basis, capacity, risk disclosure | 15% | unsupported promised lead time |
| DFM collaboration | documented bend, machining, finishing, or assembly feedback | 15% | quotes without acknowledging critical interfaces |
| Commercial completeness | pricing, NRE/tooling, MOQ, freight/tax assumptions, validity | 10% | material quote exclusions undisclosed |
| Communication / change control | named engineering contact and alternate process approval route | 15% | unapproved substitutions permitted |

Use this scorecard as a comparative decision aid, not an automatic supplier-approval mechanism. Connector, safety, and force-sensing sources require technical-owner review regardless of score.
