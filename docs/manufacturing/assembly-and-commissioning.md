# Assembly and commissioning (manufacturer-led)

1. Perform independent design and safety review; resolve items in `unresolved-design-items.md`.
2. Procure controlled components and fabricate released custom parts; inspect incoming parts.
3. Assemble base, guarded motion system, fixture/tray, and electrical enclosure to the manufacturer’s controlled work instructions.
4. Validate protective earth, branch protection, door interlock, E-stop, stored-energy behavior, and no-automatic-restart behavior before motion.
5. Calibrate home/datum frames, load cell, fixture identification, and electrical reference fixture; preserve calibration records.
6. Conduct dry-run/low-energy tests, guided-insertion trials, seating correlation, electrical-fault trials, and supervised FAT/SAT against approved criteria.
7. Train operators/maintainers and approve use only after the manufacturer’s application-specific evidence review.

This is guidance, not a machine checkout or authorization to operate.
